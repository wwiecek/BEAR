# Metapsy DOI manual-review handover

## Task

Review the 374 Metapsy reference records listed below. Each has no DOI supplied
by the dataset authors, but a Crossref candidate that failed one or more
conservative automated citation checks. The aim is to decide whether a DOI can
be attached to the exact cited article; it is not to make the review count zero.

The records are keyed by `metaid`, `study`, and exact `reference`. Eleven
database/study pairs have multiple references, so do not collapse to the study
label alone. A separate 284 records have neither a source DOI nor a usable full
reference; they are outside this handover and should remain missing unless new
source metadata are found.

## Why these records were flagged

The existing lookup queried Crossref with the full reference and required a
journal article candidate, title overlap at least 0.95, journal overlap at least
0.8, an author match, and a matching year. The first failed check is recorded
in the table. It is a screening diagnostic, not evidence that the candidate is
wrong: online-first versus issue years and source-text encoding can explain some
apparent disagreements.

| First failed check | Records |
|---|---:|
| journal | 201 |
| year | 74 |
| title | 50 |
| author | 28 |
| not a journal article | 21 |

## Instructions for the reviewer

1. Compare the exact source reference with the candidate title, journal, authors,
   year, type and DOI. Use a publisher page, Crossref record, PubMed, or another
   authoritative bibliographic record where possible. A DOI resolving is not, by
   itself, sufficient evidence.
2. For each record, record one of `replace_with_candidate`, `replace_with_other`,
   or `set_missing`. Use `replace_with_candidate` only when the candidate is the
   cited article. Use `replace_with_other` when a verified DOI differs from the
   supplied candidate; include the DOI and evidence URL. Retain `set_missing`
   where the citation is insufficient or the work has no article DOI.
3. Return a CSV with `metaid`, `study`, `reference`, `manual_decision`,
   `manual_doi`, `evidence_url`, and `manual_notes`, plus a short Markdown
   account of any systematic issue. Preserve unresolved rows rather than guessing.
4. Do not change `R/doi_lookup.R` or canonical processing during review. Identify
   repeated, evidenced failure modes separately. Accepted decisions will normally
   become a tracked Metapsy-specific mapping keyed by database, study and exact
   reference; only a recurring generic failure should motivate a shared-helper
   change.

This follows the repository identifier workflow in `doc/adding_new_datasets.md`
and the decision style used for Lang and Brodeur: candidate evidence is reviewed
first, then accepted decisions are encoded durably outside ignored lookup caches.
